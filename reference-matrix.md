# Reference matrix

## User-supplied campaign background

Job: source of protected mark, palette, circle, grid, and skyline-window geometry. Inherit the controlled
palette and structural devices; do not ask a model to redraw the mark.

## User-supplied final-poster example

Job: source of hierarchy, runner scale, outlined secondary word, distorted display block, and repeated
edge-copy behavior. Transform those relationships for localized copy; do not trace every coordinate.

## Brutalist production logic

Job: keep structure visible, copy factual, image raw, and hierarchy direct. Avoid playful neo-brutalist
cards, decorative fake data, and arbitrary distress.

Inherited: palette, central cutout, circular motion, grid, edge rhythm. Transformed: skyline becomes a
protected generative module and copy becomes locale data. Original: visible machine/human review state is
part of the poster system. Avoided: generated logos, generated type, invented official addresses, and
cultural costume.
