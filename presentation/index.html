THE 90-SECOND
INTERVIEW ROUTE.

  1. Start with one request. Paste a list of cities into Codex. Ten or one hundred locales use the same production contract.
  2. Show the fan-out. Codex turns the list into independent Comfy jobs with separate cost, retries, outputs, and QA state.
  3. Open the strategist. The LLM turns that city into a structured landmark, locale, casting, exclusion, and review brief.
  4. Trace both branches. JSON extractors route its detailed prompts into the skyline and fictional-runner generators.
  5. Point to the mask. The alpha pass makes the handoff editable instead of flattening it.
  6. Prove motion isolation. Kling sees only the runner; Bria reattaches the locked poster afterward.
  7. Close on scale and governance. One prompt can create hundreds of media pieces; humans still approve culture, language, landmark truth, and brand.
