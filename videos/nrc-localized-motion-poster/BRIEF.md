# Nike Run localization motion finish

## Intent

Finish the completed New York Comfy motion composite as a short, design-led campaign proof. Keep the
Comfy MP4 pixel-for-pixel as the media plate. Add editable, deterministic New York copy on top:
"RUN WILD", "THE CITY RUNS WITH YOU", and "FICTIONAL DEMO ROUTE · REPLACE BEFORE PRODUCTION".

The dominant motion idea is a controlled contrast: the fictional runner performs the warm-up while
the headline arrives in staggered, forceful beats and the perimeter copy moves as a continuous marquee.

## Assets

- Source video: "../../assets/generated/new-york-en/runner-warmup.mp4"
- Typeface: "../../assets/fonts/SixCaps.ttf" under SIL OFL 1.1
- Palette: "#080808", "#F5F5F2", "#B6B5B0", "#FF1616"
- Edge copy: "NIKE.COM — YOUR ONLY LIMIT IS YOU. JUST DO IT."

## Customizations

- HyperFrames motion-graphics workflow
- One full-duration composition at 1204 × 1720, 24 fps, 5.041667 seconds
- No narration, music, generated audio, or autoplay
- Staggered word/letter entrances for the headline
- Four deterministic perimeter marquee strips in Six Caps
- Sharp corners, no shadows, no gradient text, no generic UI cards

## Notes

- Independent interview prototype; not authorized by Nike or Adobe.
- The final HyperFrames output is downstream of Comfy. Comfy generates media; HyperFrames owns exact
  text, timing, and export.
- This project is the reference finish for the public multi-locale orchestrator. Each locale will receive
  its own generated media, copy payload, static poster pass, and motion finish.
