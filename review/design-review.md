# Design review — three-chair production pass

This is an independent interview prototype, not a Nike- or Adobe-authorized campaign. Paris, London, and Tokyo are complete proof outputs; all copy, casting, landmark, likeness, rights, and brand decisions remain in human-review status.

## Craft and clarity

The final posters preserve the supplied black, white, and signal-red campaign geometry while making three changes legible: skyline, fictional runner, and language. Six Caps gives every outer rail one licensed, skeletal, highly condensed voice. Tokyo uses romanized perimeter copy because Six Caps has no Japanese glyphs; the central Japanese headline remains in a native system fallback and requires regional production-font approval.

The presentation now explains the system as an actual graph rather than a gallery: protected background, skyline crop, Nano Banana edit, generated city plate, pose and wardrobe reference, new raw runner, Recraft removal, alpha mask, RGBA layer, Comfy composite, deterministic agent layer stack, and final poster.

## Uniqueness and cultural responsibility

The distinctive move is not decorative localization. It is the controlled two-branch system: place and person can change independently while campaign geometry remains protected and every intermediate stays inspectable. The landmark and casting briefs are explicit creative hypotheses, not claims of cultural authority. Local-market reviewers must approve representation, language, landmark accuracy, and the risk of reducing a city to globally familiar icons.

## Production integrity

The saved Comfy Cloud workflow uses Nano Banana Pro for both skyline and new fictional-runner generation, Recraft background removal, explicit mask polarity, alpha export, and validation compositing. Kling 3.0 receives only the isolated runner; Bria restores the locked city plate afterward. A `SaveText` output preserves the strategist JSON for downstream agents, and the last graph section contains the three real agent-finished posters as visible preview nodes. The local proof command regenerates the graph JSON, skyline crop, alpha masks, final posters, workflow validation, and QA report.

The transparent cutout is the primary pose and wardrobe reference; the green-screen source remains a documented fallback. The system does not claim the fictional personas, creative copy, or trademarks are cleared for production use.

## Current decision

The three-city proof is presentation-ready as a governed workflow demonstration. It is not campaign-ready. A real deployment would replace draft casting briefs with locally approved casting criteria, use market-approved production fonts, run native-speaker review, verify landmark and transit facts, and obtain brand, talent, and rights approvals.
