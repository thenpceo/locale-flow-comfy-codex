# Style DNA — controlled campaign brutalism

Dominant style: Brutalism. Secondary influence: Modular Typography, limited to headline behavior and
repeated edge copy.

## Invariants

- Exposed black field, hard white type, and one warning-red channel.
- Uncompromising central runner crop with no cosmetic photo smoothing.
- One circular motion device and one rectilinear grid counterweight.
- Campaign facts and review state remain actual information, never decorative metadata.
- Official supplied mark stays outside all generative operations.

## Grounded cues

- Public-address condensed type and abrupt scale changes.
- Photocopy-like monochrome portrait treatment.
- Visible production logic: repeated copy, crop edges, version and review status.

## Anti-cliches

- No tourist-poster collage or flag-as-decoration treatment.
- No faux cultural pattern, sacred symbol, national costume, or identity stereotype.
- No generic AI neon, random glitch, fake coordinates, or invented official event details.

## Variable axes

- City skyline silhouette inside the protected lower crop.
- Writing system and market-reviewed display voice.
- Draft headline and support copy.

## Permitted secondary collisions

- Headline letters may act as a repeatable vertical module.
- Edge copy may become a modular frame, but it must carry real prototype/legal status.

This is not a generic sports-social template or an imitation of a specific living designer.
